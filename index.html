Perfect Weight · Android

Privacy, plainly stated.

Privacy policy · Last updated October 9, 2026

Your diary, weigh-ins, profile and health data stay on your device. Perfect Weight has no account system, advertising, analytics or tracking.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the information you enter, including your profile and goals, food diary, exercise, water, weigh-ins, notes, waist measurements and settings. If you grant Health Connect access, the app can read steps, active calories and weight for its estimates and charts. This information is processed on your device and is not sent to the developer.

Label photos and barcode-camera frames are processed on your device and are not uploaded. The app may keep a local backup in your Documents/PerfectWeight folder. Android may also back up app data to your Google account according to your device backup settings; Google handles that backup under its own policies.

Food search and barcode lookups

If you configure a shared food server and leave shared-database search enabled, food search words are sent to that server to return matching foods. Search words are processed to answer the request and are not stored. A barcode lookup for a product not found on your device may send that barcode to the configured food server and then to Open Food Facts. Barcodes identify products, not you.

The server receives your IP address while handling a request. The developer-operated server keeps IP addresses in memory for up to one hour for abuse prevention and does not store access logs. If you configure a different server, that server's operator controls its handling of request data. Open Food Facts handles information it receives under its own privacy practices.

Products you choose to share

When creating a product for a barcode that is not already in the shared database, you may choose to share it. If you do, the product name, brand, barcode, nutrition values per 100 g, app version and any optional note you provide are sent to the developer-operated food server and kept in a review queue until reviewed. No account or personal profile is attached. If approved, the product becomes part of the shared food database and is made available to other app users.

Information you choose to send

Backups and exports are sent only to the destination you select. If you send feedback, your email app sends it to the developer. The developer uses feedback to respond and improve the app.

Retention and deletion

Your app data remains on your device until you delete it in Settings or uninstall the app. Deleting app data does not remove backup files you saved separately or data included in your platform backup. You can request removal of a product you submitted by emailing the address below; because submissions are not linked to an account, the developer can identify the product but not the person who submitted it.

Children

Perfect Weight is not intended for children under 18. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

Any changes will be published on this page with an updated date.
